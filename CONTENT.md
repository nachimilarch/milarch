## Milarch Tech — Digital Solutions

**Positioning:** Engineering high-performance web ecosystems that power pharma brands, healthcare platforms, and modern businesses.

### Service categories

1. **Pharmaceutical Brand Websites** — Custom websites for pharma brands, product detail pages, HCP portals, and patient education platforms. Built for trust, compliance awareness, and conversion.
2. **WhatsApp Chatbot Integration** — AI-powered WhatsApp bots for patient queries, product FAQs, doctor detailing support, and appointment scheduling. Integrated with Twilio and custom backends.
3. **Healthcare Web Applications** — Full-stack web apps for clinic management, prescription tracking, patient onboarding, and telemedicine workflows.
4. **E-Commerce & D2C Platforms** — Direct-to-consumer storefronts for pharmaceutical and wellness brands — product catalogues, secure checkout, prescription upload, and order tracking.
5. **SEO & Digital Marketing for Pharma** — Search engine optimisation, content marketing, and performance campaigns targeting healthcare professionals and patients in Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian cities.
6. **API & Backend Development** — RESTful APIs, database architecture, and backend services for healthcare platforms — built with Node.js, Next.js, and PostgreSQL.

### Real products, in production (case studies)

- **LadderStep** — *HR, Recruitment & Outreach SaaS.* Full-stack hiring platform with candidate and company portals, cold outreach campaigns (email + WhatsApp), interview and offer management, training services, and placement-fee billing.
- **VaartaBot** — *WhatsApp Business Platform.* Multi-tenant WhatsApp automation for businesses — visual conversation flow builder, broadcast campaigns, template management, and CRM-integrated lead capture, powering outreach for Milarch's own products and client CRMs alike.
- **Cipher** — *Field Force & Billing App.* Mobile and web platform running Milarch Pharma's own sales team — daily call reports, attendance, expense claims, RCPA tracking, and billing, with role-based admin oversight.
- **CertifyBusiness CRM** — *Outbound CRM for Compliance Services.* Multi-channel CRM for lead generation and client management — contact lists, email and WhatsApp campaigns, reply tracking, and pipeline management for a business compliance and certification firm.
