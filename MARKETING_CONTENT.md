## Post 2 — Case study: VaartaBot

Most "WhatsApp automation" pitches are a chatbot that answers 3 FAQs and falls apart the moment a real customer asks something off-script.

We built VaartaBot differently — as a multi-tenant platform, not a single bot:

→ A visual flow builder so non-technical teams can design conversation logic themselves
→ Broadcast campaigns for announcements and promotions
→ CRM-integrated lead capture — every conversation becomes a tracked lead, not a lost chat log
→ Template management built around WhatsApp's actual Business API rules (not workarounds that get your number banned)

It's live, multi-tenant, and running real businesses today — including parts of our own outreach for other Milarch products.

If your team is manually replying to the same WhatsApp questions 40 times a day, this is the fix.

→ Interview and offer management with a real audit trail
→ Training services and placement-fee billing built in, not bolted on

It's a full-stack HR & recruitment SaaS, not a CRM with a recruitment label stuck on it.

If you're running a hiring or staffing business and still stitching together 5 tools to do what should be one, let's talk.

We built a CRM for exactly this — for a business compliance and certification firm:

→ Email *and* WhatsApp campaigns from one system
→ Reply tracking so nothing falls through
→ A real pipeline view — not a spreadsheet pretending to be one

Multi-channel outbound, one place to manage it.

If your team's lead follow-up depends on someone remembering to check three inboxes, this is the kind of system that fixes it.
